How we work

Official sources

INSEE, IGN, DGFiP, the SIRENE and BODACC registers, under the Licence Ouverte. No bought or scraped data.

Every number explained

Every figure shows its source, date and method. Estimates are labelled as such.

No personal data

We keep no director’s or sole trader’s name, and honour records not open for distribution.
